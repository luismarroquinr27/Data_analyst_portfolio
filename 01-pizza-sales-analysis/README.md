# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 🎯 Business Problem
A pizza restaurant needed to identify sales patterns to optimize its menu, staffing schedules, and pricing strategy.

## 🔍 Process

1. Cleaned and prepared 21,350 sales records in Excel.
2. Built pivot tables to explore sales by day, hour, category, and size.
3. Designed an interactive Power BI dashboard with executive KPIs.
4. Extracted business insights and actionable recommendations.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Orders are **highest on weekends**, especially Friday and Saturday evenings.
- **Peak hours:** 12–1 PM and 5–8 PM.
- The **Classic** category contributes the most to sales and total orders.
- **Larger pizza sizes** generate the highest revenue.
- **Classic Deluxe and Chicken pizzas** are the top sellers.
- The **Brie Carré** has the lowest performance in both orders and revenue.

## ✅ Recommendations

1. Schedule more staff during peak hours (12–1 PM and 5–8 PM).
2. Promote Classic Deluxe and Chicken pizzas in combo offers.
3. Re-evaluate the Brie Carré — consider removing or repositioning it.
4. Offer promotions on weekdays to balance demand.
